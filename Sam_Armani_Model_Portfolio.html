02 / Strategy

Invest where the
decision improves.

I evaluate opportunities through three lenses, then revisit priorities as evidence develops.

VALUE

A meaningful decision

A clear business owner, an actionable output and a measurable improvement over the current approach.

FEASIBILITY

A credible path to use

Usable data, an appropriate baseline and a realistic route into an operational workflow.

ACCOUNTABILITY

A sustainable commitment

Maintenance effort, decision risk and ownership that fit the expected value over time.

04 / Lifecycle governance

Clear gates.
Accountable decisions.

My recommended framework makes the evidence, decision rights and next step visible at every stage.

01

Frame

Define the decision, business owner, value hypothesis and acceptable risk.

Output: a scoped use case
02

Validate

Compare to a baseline, test relevant segments and review privacy and decision impacts.

Output: evidence for readiness
03

Release

Record approval, version and lineage. Define monitoring, fallback and rollout conditions.

Output: an approved release
04

Operate

Review alerts and outcomes. Assign incident owners and record interventions.

Output: an accountable decision log
05

Renew or retire

Reassess value and risk. Revalidate changes or decommission models that no longer serve the decision.

Output: a lifecycle decision

Proportionate to the decision. Set review depth, monitoring cadence and intervention thresholds to match business impact, data availability and the consequences of getting it wrong.
